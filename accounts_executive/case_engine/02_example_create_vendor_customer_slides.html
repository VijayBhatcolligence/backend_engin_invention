Controls · 78 stop rules + 8 warnings + 6 refusals

What stops a bad account from going live

Identity

Is it the right party?

PAN and GSTIN format · GSTIN belongs to this PAN · state matches the GSTIN · PIN has 6 digits

Duplicates

Do we already have it?

GSTIN already our vendor → form returned · bank account used by another party → returned · account name taken

Compliance

MSME and 43B(h)

MSME vendor above 45 credit days → stop · category says MSME but MSME = No → stop · Udyam format

Bank

Will payments reach them?

Penny-drop must be run · name mismatch → call-back required · a call-back number taken from an email is refused

Every rule runs on the server, not only on screen, and has a code (e.g. SUB-17 = MSME credit days). Each rule and each refusal has its own test: 91 tests.

Actions and gates, section E · rule tests, section R7
Outside checks

Confirmed with the GST portal and the bank

GST portal

Is the GSTIN live?

Active → ok. Suspended → warning. Cancelled → stop, form returned. The portal's name is compared with ours (80% match).

Penny-drop

₹1 sent to the account

The bank returns the holder's name. Pass at 80% name match. Change the bank details and it must be run again.

Call-back

A person confirms

Needed when the name does not match. Who was called and on which number is recorded at Submit.

If the service is downThen
Timeout 10 s, 2 retriesThe AE sees "service unavailable" and tries later; the request is unchanged
AuditEvery request and answer is kept, with PAN and account numbers masked
TestingA fake service with fixed answers, so tests never call the real portal or bank

The accounting view

A party is a sub-ledger account under a control account

No journal entry

Opening an account moves no money. Balances are unchanged and sub-ledgers still tie to the control accounts.

The AE never creates a header

"Under (header)" comes from the category mapping, owned by the Accounts Manager.

One party, many roles

A party is recorded once for the group; it can be our vendor and our customer, with one account on each side.

Safe in production

Failures and personal data are planned for

If something fails

An action failsNothing saved; pressing again is safe
An automatic step failsRetried 5 times; then in the AE's exception queue
Chain stops halfwayDone steps stay; retry continues; nothing reversed
Stuck over 2 hoursAccounts Manager alerted
Wrong value went liveFixed by a new approved action; history kept

Personal and bank data

9 sensitive fieldsPAN, email, phone, bank account, call-back number, check details
